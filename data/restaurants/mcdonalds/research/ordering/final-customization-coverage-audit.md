- Breakfast sandwiches: bread, butter, egg, sausage/steak/Canadian bacon, breakfast sauce, spicy sauce, onions, salt, and other parent-specific portions without exact full-macro contexts.

- Fruit & Maple Oatmeal: fruit/raisin blend, diced apples, and cream; ordering calories exist but full macros do not.
- Sausage Burrito: Hot and Mild Picante Salsa. Ketchup Packet and No Sauce remain available in the required slot.
- Snack Wraps: ranch/spicy sauce, lettuce, and shredded cheddar/jack cheese. Bacon and two tomato slices are enabled.
- Nuggets/Strips: Tangy BBQ, Sweet N Sour, Honey Packet, and Hot Picante Salsa pending exact catalog nutrition.
- McCafé: all milk/cream, syrup/flavor, sweetener, espresso, foam, ice, topping, and removal actions. Captures do not establish exact size-specific four-macro deltas or pump/milk quantities.

## Remaining data and product issues

- Menu provenance: the upstream review still contains 61 public-menu records without a DNA item ID (6 meal/combo pages, 11 limited/promotional pages, 28 size/variant pages, and 16 standalone pages). They remain explicitly unresolved rather than guessed.
- Nutrition: the hidden modifiers above have calories-only labels or parent/size conflicts, not authoritative protein/carbs/fat deltas.
- Beverages: 64 of 70 combo drink records are reconciled; six audited ambiguous drink records remain excluded from automatic combo mapping.
- Combos and carts: no known regression remains. Existing entrée combo builders, fixed bundles, selected variant identities, structured modifier IDs, and edit restoration pass the test suite. Source-only meal records intentionally remain relationship data.
- Images: validated items retain official or same-family fallbacks; records without a loadable exact/same-family image continue to use the application placeholder. No image identity was inferred during this rollout.
- Validator: the generated McDonald's dataset currently reports zero errors and zero warnings.

## Completion decision

The McDonald's import is complete for the currently captured dataset under the conservative policy used here: every captured modifier with reliable ordering identity and full macro nutrition is enabled, and unresolved options are explicitly hidden and documented. It is not “complete” in the broader sense of enabling every live regional/seasonal modifier or resolving the 61 upstream records that lack DNA identities.
